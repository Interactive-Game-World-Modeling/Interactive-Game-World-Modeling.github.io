About

Creating a playable game world requires visual content, game rules, and player actions to work together. This workshop brings together agentic game development, game-ready 3D/4D generation, and interactive world models around a shared goal: turning design intent into playable experiences.

Topics

  • Code-to-visual generation and LLMs

    Agents that turn game briefs into executable, playtested projects.

  • 3D/4D generation

    Game-ready assets, characters, terrain, and dynamic scenes.

  • Interactive game world models

    Neural game engines, action-conditioned generation, and real-time control.

  • Data, evaluation, and human interaction

    Design intent, playability, reliability, and tools to revise worlds.

Call for Participation

Proposed tracks. A team may enter any combination. Calls open from January 2027.

  • Research Track

    Non-archival long papers (up to 8 pages) and short papers (up to 4 pages) in CVPR format, with double-blind review on OpenReview. Accepted papers over 4 pages count as prior publications under CVPR guidelines.

    Papers due May 1, 2027

  • AI for Games Hackathon

    Build with AI for games, mainly with 3D/4D generation and code agents: executable games, video-based worlds, 3D/4D environments, or hybrids. Online, with no paper or public source code required. Up to eight finalists demo at the workshop.

    Projects due May 1, 2027

  • Shared Task

    Recreate playable games from AAA-quality gameplay video with any method. The Game Recreation Benchmark has about 20 tasks from licensed games and tests each build's mechanics, visuals, and response to input, with a public leaderboard.

    Entries due May 1, 2027
